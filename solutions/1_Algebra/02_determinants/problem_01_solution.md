
## Exercise 1 — Solution

For a 2×2 matrix, the determinant formula is:

$$
\det(A)=ad-bc
$$

**Matrix A:**

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}3&-2\\5&4\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\det(A)=3\cdot4-(-2)\cdot5
$$

$$
=12+10=\boxed{22}
$$

**Matrix B:**

$$
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&7\\2&14\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\det(B)=1\cdot14-7\cdot2
$$

$$
=14-14=\boxed{0}
$$

**Conclusion:**

Matrix A is invertible because its determinant is not zero.

Matrix B is not invertible because its determinant is zero.
