
## Exercise 4 — Solution

Given matrix:

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
2&4&1\\
0&-3&5\\
0&0&7
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Step 1: Identify the matrix**

This is an upper triangular matrix because all elements below the main diagonal are zero.

**Step 2: Apply the determinant rule**

For any triangular matrix, the determinant is the product of the diagonal elements.

$$
\det(A)=a_{11}\cdot a_{22}\cdot a_{33}
$$

For this matrix:

$$
\det(A)=2\cdot(-3)\cdot7
$$

$$
=-6\cdot7
$$

$$
\boxed{\det(A)=-42}
$$

**Conclusion:**

The determinant is **-42**.

For any upper or lower triangular matrix, we multiply the diagonal elements to find the determinant. The other terms in the determinant calculation contain zeros, so they do not affect the result.
